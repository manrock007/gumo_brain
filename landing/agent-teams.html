Composition

How a team is composed.

Every CtrLoop team maps to the way people already organize work - someone assigns and checks it, specialists do it, a reviewer gates it, and a human owns the calls that matter.

◆

Manager agent

Breaks the workflow into steps, assigns them to the right role and checks the work comes back complete.

◇

Role agents

Specialist agents that do the actual work of the function - each scoped to one job and its tools.

◈

Reviewer agent

Gates quality and safety before anything ships - running evaluation and policy checks on every output.

◉

Human-in-the-loop

A person approves the calls that matter - the gate where judgment, accountability and sign-off live.

Why teams

Why teams beat single agents.

A lone agent is a feature. A team is a worker - it coordinates, holds a line on quality, and gets better at the job over time.

Coordination

Agents hand off on shared context instead of restarting cold - so a multi-step workflow stays coherent from first step to last.

Reliability

Contracts between roles stop drift - each handoff has a defined shape, so one agent's output is another's dependable input.

Self-improvement

The loop compounds - traces and outcomes feed back, and the team beats its own baseline run after run.

The foundation

Built on the CtrLoop platform.

Every team runs on one operating layer - shared context, an execution engine, and a control plane of contracts, evaluation and governance - so a new team reuses the permissions, integrations and audit you've already built.
