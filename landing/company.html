Our vision

Every enterprise will run on AI - if whole teams can adopt it, not just individuals.

Most AI stalls at the pilot: one clever agent, one power user, no path to the rest of the team. We're building the operating layer that lets an entire function - people and agents together - adopt AI and compound at it. We ship the way we ask our customers to: forward-deployed, inside the team, in production. A team out of Amazon Alexa and MakeMyTrip - AI at scale, and P&Ls that shipped.
