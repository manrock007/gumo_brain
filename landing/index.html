FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is CtrLoop?

CtrLoop is the Work OS for enterprise teams - a unified operating layer where your people and autonomous AI agents run work together on one shared context, so whole teams adopt AI and every loop compounds.

How is it different from a single AI agent?

Instead of one standalone agent, we stand up the whole team - every role plus its agents - and own the loop (plan, execute, launch, feedback, iterate) so quality compounds run after run instead of resetting.

Which industries do you serve?

Travel, retail, NBFC / BFSI and government - plus enterprise functions like growth, pricing, operations and finance.

Is CtrLoop secure and governed?

Yes. Every action runs with contracts, evaluation, RBAC and full audit, and CtrLoop deploys on-prem or in your own VPC - so a regulated enterprise can grant autonomy safely.
