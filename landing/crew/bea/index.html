Questions, answered

Which internal systems can Bea connect to?+

Anything with an API, a database, a file export or an inbox - typically ERP and accounting, CRM, inventory and order systems, helpdesks, email and shared sheets. Connectors are set up during onboarding.

What external data can Bea collect?+

Public market data - prices, rank, stock, rates, availability, reviews and listings - from marketplaces, OTAs, quick commerce, competitor sites and any site you need.

How fresh is the data?+

As fresh as the job needs: hourly or faster for fast-moving markets, daily for slower ones, or on demand. Every record carries its timestamp and source.

Can I use Bea's data on its own?+

Yes. Bea's data layer is available standalone through an API and MCP, or it can feed Otto and Dash.

Where does the data live?+

In our cloud, your cloud or on-premise, with role-based access.
