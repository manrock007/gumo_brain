Questions, answered

Which channels and systems can Dash work in?+

Email, WhatsApp, Slack and Teams, plus your CRM, ERP and inventory, seller panels, channel managers, ad platforms, helpdesk and calendar. Connections are set up during onboarding.

Can Dash send messages to my customers?+

Yes - from your own email and WhatsApp business accounts, using approved templates and tone, within quiet hours you set.

What happens if nobody replies?+

Dash follows the plan: waits, nudges on another channel, then escalates to the owner you choose.

How do guardrails work?+

You set limits and approval rules per action. Dash never steps outside them, every step is logged, and most changes can be undone in one click.

Can Dash run fully on its own?+

Only where you allow it. Autonomy is earned task by task - you keep the gate.
