Questions, answered

How is Otto different from a dashboard?+

A dashboard shows data and waits for someone to read it. Otto reads it for you, explains what changed and why, and decides the next move.

When does Otto hand over to a human?+

Whenever the stakes are high, confidence is low, or you've asked to approve that type of decision. You set the line for each decision type.

Can Otto explain its decisions?+

Yes. Every recommendation comes with what changed, why it matters, the expected impact and the risk.

Does Otto act on its own?+

Otto decides; Dash acts - automatically only where you have allowed it, inside your guardrails.
