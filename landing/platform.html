Why one layer

The advantages of a unified layer.

Model optionality

Nothing you build is locked to a model - each task routes to whichever model fits, on cost and quality.

Accelerated building

Permissions, integrations and audit are built once and reused - a new use case starts from what you already have.

Shared context

Humans and agents work off one context - no re-explaining the business for every task.

Centralized governance

One place defines who and what can access which data and actions - enforced across every agent.

Compounding intelligence

Every run improves the shared system - the loop is the moat, not any single output.

Flexible deployment

Multi-tenant SaaS, single-tenant, or in your own VPC across AWS, Azure and GCP.
